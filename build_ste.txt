Sol.1.(b) (2) - were ready for the sudden fire drill
'Were' should be replaced with 'was'. When 'neither... nor'
connects two subjects, the verb agrees with the subject closest
to it. In this case, 'the teacher' is singular, so the verb should be
singular ('was'). Hence, 'was ready for the sudden fire drill' is the
most appropriate answer.
Sol.2.(b) (2) - remains a contentious subject
‘Remains’ must be replaced with ‘remain’. The subject of the
sentence is ‘The profound implications’ (plural). The phrase ‘a
field still largely inscrutable to the uninitiated’ provides additional
information about ‘quantum theories’ but does not change the
number of the main subject. Therefore, a plural verb is required.
Hence, ‘remain a contentious subject’ is the most appropriate
answer.
Sol.3.(b) (2) - ranging from sustainable infrastructure to social
equity, often demands
‘Demands’ must be replaced with ‘demand’. The subject of the
sentence is ‘The myriad challenges’ (plural). The phrase
‘confronting modern urban planners’ is a participial phrase
modifying the subject and does not change its number.
Therefore, a plural verb is required. Hence, ‘often demand’ is the
most appropriate correction.
Sol.4.(c) (3) - before the deadline, and they shall submits
‘Submits’ must be replaced with ‘submit’.
When using modal verbs like 'shall' (or 'will', 'can', 'may', etc.), the
main verb that follows must be in its base form. Therefore, 'shall
submit' is the correct form. Hence, 'and they shall submit' is the
most appropriate correction.
Sol.5.(c) (3)
According to the 'Rule of Parallelism', two or more words or
phrases in a list must be in the same form. In the given
sentence, "increasing membership" and "improving
communication channels" are gerund phrases. "To foster a
sense of community" is an infinitive phrase. To maintain
parallelism, the third item should also be a gerund phrase.
Hence, "and fostering a sense of community" is the most
appropriate answer.
Sol.6.(c) (3)
According to the 'Subject-Verb
Agreement Rule', a singular subject always takes a singular verb
and a plural subject always takes a plural verb. In the given
sentence, the main subject is "The unique challenges," which is
plural. Therefore, the verb should also be plural ("make") instead
of singular ("makes"). Hence, "make its successful completion"
is the most appropriate answer.
Sol.7.(b) (2) and promoting critical scholarship
‘Both … and’ is a fixed conjunction pair and the same part of
speech should be used after each. Therefore, ‘promoting’ must
be replaced with ‘promote’ to match the form of the verb
‘preserve’. Hence, ‘and promote critical scholarship’ is the most
appropriate answer.
Sol.8.(c) (3) - are increasingly becoming a topic
‘Are’ must be replaced with ‘is’. The subject of the sentence is
‘The profound impact’ (singular). Therefore, a singular verb is
required. Hence, ‘is increasingly becoming a topic’ is the most
appropriate answer.
Sol.9.(c) (3) - but also it was thoroughly tested ‘Not only … but
also’ is a fixed conjunction pair and the same part of speech is
used after them. As there is an adjective after ‘not only’,, we
need to use an adjective after ‘but also’. Therefore, ‘it was’ must
be removed. Hence, ‘but also thoroughly tested’ is the most
